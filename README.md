# User-Profile-Analysis
This project analyses the profiles and behaviour of active premium users on a music streaming platform. It identifies non-premium users with similar characteristics who could be targeted through tailored marketing campaigns to improve premium subscription conversion.

# Project Title
Identifying the profile of active premuim users on a music subscription platform

# Business Context
Premium user conversion has stalled business would like to target new users, however targeting needs to be specific, based on user profile, business wants to target specific free users who match premium user context

# Dataset overview
Data tracks users demographic profile and listening behaviour. Data points include ('user_id', 'country', 'age', 'signup_date', 'subscription_type', 'subscription_status', 'months_inactive', 'inactive_3_months_flag','ad_interaction','ad_conversion_to_subscription','music_suggestion_rating_1_to_5', 'avg_listening_hours_per_week','favorite_genre', 'most_liked_feature', 'desired_future_feature','primary_device', 'playlists_created', 'avg_skips_per_day')

# Active Premium User
Premium users who:
  1.were not inactive during the past three months; and
  2.had no more than one inactive month overall.

# Analysis approach
The analysis was structured around three main areas:

  ## 1. Active Premium User Profile

    The demographic and geographic profile of Active Premium Users was assessed using:

    Age
    Country or location
    Representation of different age groups across countries

  ## 2. Listening Behaviour

    Listening behaviour was examined to understand:
      The primary devices users listen on
      The music genres users prefer
      How long users listen for
      Differences in average and median listening time across user groups

  ## 3. Cross-Sectional Analysis

    Cross-sectional comparisons were conducted to identify patterns between user characteristics and       listening behaviour.

    Country-level analysis
      Which age groups are represented in each country?
      Which devices are most commonly used in each country?
      Which music genres are most popular in each country?
      How long do users in each country listen for?
      
    Age-group analysis
      Which devices are most popular among each age group?
      Which music genres are preferred by each age group?
      How long does each age group listen for?

# Key findings

A typical Active Premium User is a working-age adult who listens for approximately 10 hours per week, uses a range of devices and consumes a diverse mix of music genres. Their engagement level is broadly consistent regardless of age or country, while device and genre preferences provide the clearest opportunities for more targeted segmentation.

## 1. Listening Engagement
Active Premium Users listen for around 10 hours per week on average. Listening time remains broadly consistent   across countries and age groups, indicating that Premium engagement is not concentrated within one particular    market or age segment.

## 2. Device Usage
Tablets are the most commonly used devices among Active Premium Users, while desktops are the least used.        However, device preferences vary slightly by country, and no single device strongly dominates across all         markets or age groups.

## 3. Genre Preferences
Electronic music is the most listened-to genre overall, while Pop is the least listened to. However, genre       preferences are widely distributed, with no single genre strongly dominating the Premium audience.

## 4. Genre Preferences
Countries show some variation in their leading devices and preferred genres, but these differences are           relatively small. Overall, Active Premium Users display more similarities than differences in their listening    behaviour across markets.

## 5. Differences by Age
Device usage and listening time are broadly consistent across age groups. Genre preferences show more            noticeable differences, suggesting that age may be more useful for understanding music taste than for            predicting how long or on which device Premium users listen.
